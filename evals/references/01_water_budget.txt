A municipal water budget has four stages: demand estimation by connection
category, supply estimation from reservoir inflow, reconciliation of the two
through restrictions or reserves, and publication. Operating and capital costs
are reported separately because they are funded differently, and mixing them
hides tariff shortfalls. Non-revenue water and cost per kilolitre delivered are
the two figures that matter most, and the budget is revised twice a year - once
technically, once politically.
