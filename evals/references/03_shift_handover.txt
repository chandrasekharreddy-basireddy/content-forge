Shift handovers fail for three reasons: wrong timing, wrong content, or no record.
The useful window is the last twenty minutes of the shift, while the plant is
still running. The content that matters is what has changed, what is abnormal and
what the next shift must not touch, not a summary of the whole shift. A written
sheet signed by both shifts, with sections for changes, abnormal conditions and
hold points, is the only version that survives an incident review. The commonest
improvement is simply fixing a scheduled slot both shifts plan around.
