India's installed solar capacity reached 174.55 GW AC by October 2026, making it the third-largest solar producer in the world after China and the United States and one of the fastest adopters. The country hosts several of the world's largest solar parks, including Bhadla in Rajasthan and Pavagada in Karnataka. Growth has been driven by government initiatives, 100% foreign direct investment in renewables, and more than a hundred dedicated solar parks established to make land available to developers.
