The sharing of the Kaveri's water has been a long conflict between Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, rooted in agreements of 1892 and 1924 between the Kingdom of Mysore and the Madras Presidency. The 802 km river rises at Talakaveri in Karnataka and crosses into Tamil Nadu, with a basin of 44,000 sq km in Tamil Nadu and 32,000 sq km in Karnataka. Two decades of negotiation failed; a tribunal constituted in 1990 issued an interim award in 1991 and a final allocation in 2007, which the Supreme Court revised in 2018.
