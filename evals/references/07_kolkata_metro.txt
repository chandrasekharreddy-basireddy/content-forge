The Kolkata Metro, opened in 1984, is South Asia's first rapid transit system and India's fourth largest and fourth busiest. It runs five colour-coded lines over 73.42 km with 55 operational stations, mixing underground, at-grade and elevated sections on both broad and standard gauge, electrified at 750 V DC third rail. It is the only metro in the country controlled entirely by Indian Railways, and carries more than 800,000 passengers on around 300 daily trips.
