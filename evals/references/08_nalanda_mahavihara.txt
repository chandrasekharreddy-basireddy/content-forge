Nalanda was a Buddhist mahavihara in medieval Magadha, near modern Rajgir in Bihar, operating for almost a thousand years from 427 CE until around 1400 CE. It is widely regarded as one of the greatest centres of learning in the ancient world and is often described as the world's first residential university, attracting students and monks across Asia and playing a central role in the patronage of arts, culture and scholarship in the region.
