Chilika Lake is the largest brackish-water lagoon on India's east coast and the second largest coastal lagoon in the world, lying in Odisha's Puri, Khordha and Ganjam districts at the mouth of the Daya River. Its area varies with the season from about 900 to 1,165 sq km. It was among India's first two Ramsar sites, designated in 1981, supports over 225 bird species and the endangered Irrawaddy dolphin, and provides livelihoods to roughly 200,000 fisherfolk from more than 130 villages.
