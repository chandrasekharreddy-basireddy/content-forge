Indian Railways is a state-owned enterprise under the Ministry of Railways operating the national network, with a route length of 69,181 km and 135,207 km of track as of 2024, making it the fourth largest system in the world by size. About 99.1% of the broad-gauge network was electrified by October 2025, and the organisation employs more than 1.2 million people, making it one of the world's largest employers. It carries passengers and freight across broad, metre and narrow gauge routes.
