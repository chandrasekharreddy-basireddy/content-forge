The Unified Payments Interface is an Indian instant payment system built by the National Payments Corporation of India and launched in 2016, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India. It allows inter-bank person-to-person and person-to-merchant transfers from mobile devices using only a unique UPI ID, running as an open API on top of the Immediate Payment Service. Major Indian banks adopted it for their own applications, which is what carried it from a protocol into everyday retail payment.
